rv32 development examples

  lua ~/examples/hello.lua
  luac -o /tmp/hello.luac ~/examples/hello.lua
  lua /tmp/hello.luac
  c4 ~/examples/hello.c
  c4 -s ~/examples/hello.c

Lua executes in guest Linux. The Lua compiler emits Lua bytecode.
c4 runs a small C subset on its own bytecode VM. It does not emit ELF files.
Use vi to edit the examples. For POSIX programs and native ELF output, use
a host RISC-V cross toolchain and rebuild the image with your application.
